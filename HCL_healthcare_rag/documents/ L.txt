Scope



1. The policy is applicable to all employees on payrolls of HCL Technologies Ltd and its subsidiaries located in India.
2. Enrollment for "Self" is mandatory, with no option to opt-out.
3. Probationary employees on company payrolls are covered.
4. EMCP Employees: Dependents of employees transferred Onsite are covered under the Extended Medical Coverage Plan (EMCP).
5. Retiring Employees and those with 10 or more years of experience.
    * They have the option to continue coverage post-retirement by paying pro-rata premiums. Dependents declared at retirement continue coverage until September '26 with no changes, except for life-changing events. Premiums and coverage limits are as per last employee band before retirement. Coverage choice must be notified 30 days before retirement or Last Working Day (LWD).
This Policy DOES NOT Cover Specific Employee Categories

1. ESI Employees
* Employees covered under ESI are not automatically included in HCL's corporate Medical Insurance policy.
* However, they have a one-time option to enroll during the dependent declaration window or within 21 days of joining.
* If opted, the employee bears the additional annual premium cost as per policy terms.
Once enrolled, opting out is not permitted until the policy lock-in period ends in September '26.

2. Contract Employees
* Contract employees (CS, CR, CT, AT, and TS) under direct payroll are not initially covered.
* They have a one-time opportunity to join during the dependent declaration window or within 21 days of joining.
* Sum insurance coverage aligns with the employee's retired/last applicable band.
* The employee bears additional annual premium costs.
Opting out is prohibited until the policy lock-in period ends in September '26.

Medical Allowance: Domiciliary claims are not covered by this Medical Insurance policy.
Medical Insurance Portability: If you leave the organization, you have the option to port the base policy according to the terms of New India Assurance. Changes in the sum insured (SI) limit and the selection of dependents will not be permitted at the time of porting the policy. Employees must contact dryagdeep.chugh@indiainsure.com / ashish.s@indiainsure.com at least 15 days before their Last Working Day (LWD) for portability.

Top-Up Exclusions
The top-up policy becomes effective only after the employee utilizes their base sum insured.
For example: If an employee's base Sum Insured is INR 7 Lacs and they have opted for a Top-Up of INR 3 Lacs. Let's assume the employee has used INR 4 Lacs during their tenure with HCL Technologies Ltd. Then, their remaining Sum Insured is INR 3 Lacs when they leave. If the employee incurs hospitalization expenses of INR 4 Lacs after leaving HCL Technologies Ltd., they are eligible for INR 1 Lac from the Top-Up Policy. This is because the Top-Up Policy comes into effect only after the employee exhaust their remaining Sum Insured (INR 3 Lacs in this case).
Any amount beyond the limit (procedures which are capped) as defined in the GHMI/EMCP policy cannot be claimed under the OPD or Top-up Policy. Example Cataract related treatment is covered Up to INR 35,000 per eye in Group medical plan. Any expenses incurred beyond INR 35,000 cannot be claimed in OPD or Top-up policy

Policy Principle and Exclusions

Fraudulent Claims: If an employee is found guilty of making fraudulent claims under the Medical Insurance Policy and/or violates the Company's Code of Conduct, appropriate disciplinary action, including termination of employment, shall be taken against such employee. This is in accordance with the procedures set out in the Company’s ‘Code of Business Ethics and Conduct’ (COBEC) policy, which is available in the Policies Hub portal.

Principle: The Medical Insurance policy covers expenses (excluding non-medical items as specified in Annexure 3) deemed "reasonable" during the hospitalization of any dependent. Within the available treatment alternatives, the policy reimburses or pays for the most reasonable option. If an employee or dependent chooses a treatment option beyond the reasonable option, the difference in cost must be borne by the employee.

Claims Process for Top-Up - for Separated Employees
* A claim under the Top-up policy will trigger only if your total payable claim amount, either in a single event or the sum of multiple claims during the same policy period, is more than the base Sum Insured limit as per the HCL band.
* The Top-up policy will pay the payable claim value over and above the base Sum Insured limit eligibility as per the HCL band only. For example, an employee eligible for INR 5 Lacs base Sum Insured limit as per HCL band can file a claim for the payable claim amount (Claim amount – Non-payable expenses e.g., Co-pay, non-medical expenses etc.) over and above INR 5 Lacs only under the HCL Top-up policy.
* In a claim of INR 8 lacs, if the payable claim amount is INR 7 Lacs (INR 8 Lacs - Non-payable expenses e.g., Co-pay, non-medical expenses etc.), then the employee must settle the first 5 lacs, which is the base Sum Insured limit as per the HCL band and works as a threshold limit for the Top-up policy from any other personal/corporate policy or out of his/her pocket, and the remaining 2 lacs i.e., 7-5 =2 will be picked by the HCL Top-up policy.
* For the calculation of the threshold limit, if not breached in a single claim and previously during the same policy period, multiple hospitalizations have taken place for the same family members as covered under the HCL policy, then the sum of the payable claim value of these previous hospitalizations will be considered to calculate the threshold limit.
